# MatchCast synthetic dataset card

Every match, event and scene in MatchCast is generated by our own simulator from a seed. No real event, player, club or name is in the data. Real football is used only as **aggregate statistics** to calibrate the simulator (rates, shares, one fitted curve).

## Sources used for calibration

| Source | Link | Licence | Used for |

## Scene dataset

Generated by `scripts/generate_scenes.py --count 240 --seed 2026`: headless full-density matches (seeds 2026 and up), cut into 30 s windows, each labelled with one of six families by fixed rules (counter-attack, high press, possession build-up, set piece, chaotic scramble, quiet spell) with the same number of scenes kept per family. One row per event.

**Label.** `danger` is 1 when the event's team takes a shot in the 10 s after the event, otherwise 0.

**Family rules** (first match wins, per 30 s window): set piece = a corner, free kick or penalty (throw-ins do not count, they fall in most windows); counter-attack = two or more passes in the `counter` phase; chaotic scramble = four or more turnovers; high press = two or more turnovers, at least one in the opponent's third; possession build-up = six or more passes and at most one turnover; quiet spell = six events or fewer and no shot.

**Counts** (`data/scenes/v1/manifest.json`, seed 2026, 16 full 90-minute matches, 30 s windows): 240 scenes, 40 per family, 2,142 rows. Across the 16 matches the windows that fit a family were 672 set piece, 495 counter-attack, 418 possession build-up, 359 quiet spell, 291 high press and 122 chaotic scramble, so each family has room above the 40 kept; 746 windows fit none and are dropped.

**Class balance.** `danger` = 1 on 3.97% of rows (85 of 2,142). That is thin: the held-out split in the model has 4 matches and about 20 positives, so the model numbers below carry wide error.

**Known limits.** The scenes describe the simulator, not real football; the families are rule labels, not human labels; one seed block means one league of 16 fictional matches; events carry no player tracking, so pressure and shape are not features. Nothing here is real data.

## Danger model (DAT-6)

`data/databricks/train_danger.py` trains a gradient-boosted classifier for `danger`, validated by match (whole matches held out, 4 of 16). Local run on the committed scenes: held-out AUC 0.884, Brier 0.0448 against 0.0447 for always predicting the base rate. So it ranks moments well but its probabilities are no better calibrated than the base rate on this small sample, and the Verifier must not quote them as odds; a bigger scene set (more matches) is the fix. Output in `data/models/danger/v1/`: `model.onnx` (input `X`, 46 float features in the order of `model-card.json`, missing numeric fields filled with -1), `model-card.json` and `heldout-sample.json` (10 held-out rows with the training run's probability; onnxruntime reproduces them to 5e-7). Registering it in Unity Catalog and the Databricks job run need the Mac mini and stay **Planned**. Install the extras with `uv sync --group ml`.
