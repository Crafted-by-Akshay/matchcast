# MatchCast synthetic dataset card

Every match, event and scene in MatchCast is generated by our own simulator from a seed. No real event, player, club or name is in the data. Real football is used only as **aggregate statistics** to calibrate the simulator (rates, shares, one fitted curve).

| Source | Link | Licence | Used for |

## Scene dataset

Generated by `scripts/generate_scenes.py --count 240 --seed 2026`: headless full-density matches (seeds 2026 and up), cut into 30 s windows, each labelled with one of six families by fixed rules (counter-attack, high press, possession build-up, set piece, chaotic scramble, quiet spell) with the same number of scenes kept per family. One row per event.

**Label.** `danger` is 1 when the event's team takes a shot in the 10 s after the event, otherwise 0.

**Family rules** (first match wins, per 30 s window): set piece = a corner, free kick or penalty (throw-ins do not count, they fall in most windows); counter-attack = two or more passes in the `counter` phase; chaotic scramble = four or more turnovers; high press = two or more turnovers, at least one with the engine's `ownX` (the turning-over side's distance from its own goal line, in metres) at 66 or more; possession build-up = six or more passes and at most one turnover; quiet spell = six events or fewer and no shot.

**Counts** (`data/scenes/v1/manifest.json`, seed 2026, 16 full 90-minute matches, 30 s windows): 240 scenes, 40 per family, 2,142 rows. Across the 16 matches the windows that fit a family were 672 set piece, 495 counter-attack, 418 possession build-up, 359 quiet spell, 291 high press and 122 chaotic scramble, so each family has room above the 40 kept; 746 windows fit none and are dropped.

**Class balance.** `danger` = 1 on 3.97% of rows (85 of 2,142). That is thin: the held-out split has 4 matches and 25 positives, so the model numbers below carry wide error.

**Selection caveat.** A scene's family is decided from the whole 30 s window, including events after each row, and the 746 windows that fit no family are dropped. A quiet spell has no shot by definition, so its early rows are almost all `danger` = 0, and a set-piece window is chosen because a corner or free kick may come later in it. Models trained and scored on these scenes therefore overstate how well they would do on a live event stream, and the family is not a feature.

**Known limits.** The scenes describe the simulator, not real football; the families are rule labels, not human labels; one seed block means one league of 16 fictional matches; events carry no player tracking, so pressure and shape are not features. Nothing here is real data. Byte-identical output holds for the same simulator build and pyarrow version (the Parquet footer records pyarrow's version); two fresh runs here matched byte for byte.

## Danger model (DAT-6)

`data/databricks/train_danger.py` trains a gradient-boosted classifier for `danger`, validated by match (whole matches held out, 4 of 16). Local run on the committed scenes: held-out AUC 0.884, Brier 0.0430 against 0.0447 for always predicting the base rate. It ranks moments well and is only slightly better than the base rate on probability, and it is overconfident at the top end (the 0.9 to 1.0 bin predicts 0.97 and observed 0.60, n = 5; the 0.8 to 0.9 bin predicts 0.84 and observed 0.50, n = 2). The Verifier must not quote the probabilities as odds, and the AUC carries the selection caveat above. A bigger scene set (more matches) is the fix.

Features (`model-card.json`, 47 floats in that order): the nine numeric fields, `type=` one-hots (pass, turnover, shot, take_on, slide_tackle, aerial_duel, foul, corner, freekick, throw_in, goal), `phase=` and `kind=` one-hots. `t_rel_s` (time into the offline window) is not a feature, because a live event has no such window. A missing numeric field is filled with -1. Output in `data/models/danger/v1/`: `model.onnx` (input `X`), `model-card.json` and `heldout-sample.json` (10 held-out rows, the highest-scoring five and five more spread through the rest, with the training run's probability; onnxruntime reproduces them to 5e-7). Registering it in Unity Catalog and the Databricks job run need the Mac mini and stay **Planned**. Install the extras with `uv sync --group ml`.
