---

## Styles

Casual keeps lines short and light, with at most one number. Analyst writes longer lines with up to six numbers and shows the evidence. Broadcast writes lines meant to be read aloud, with at most two numbers.

## Watch modes

Analyst mode shows detail, Casual mode shows the story, My club follows one side, Player focus follows one player, and One metric follows a single statistic through the match. Your choice is remembered in your browser.

## Languages

The first release is English, with Spanish and Hindi through translation. After translation the Verifier checks the numbers and names again, because a translation must not change a fact.
