---
title: What Simulated means
---

## Everything is synthetic

The clubs, players, stadiums and matches in MatchCast are fictional. Matches are produced by a seeded simulator, so the same seed always plays out the same way. No real player, club or broadcast footage is used, and the numbers should not be read as statistics about real football.

## Why that matters

Because the simulator is the source of truth, every claim can be checked against the event log it produced. The simulator is calibrated only on aggregate figures, such as typical goals and shots per match, so that its matches feel realistic.

## The Simulated label

Screens that show simulated content carry a Simulated label so nobody mistakes it for a real fixture.
