## What MatchCast is

MatchCast turns a stream of football match events into live commentary, statistics and a written recap. Four agents share the work: the Producer decides which moments matter, the Analyst measures them, the Narrator writes the line, and the Verifier checks it before anyone sees it.

## Three views

Watch is for fans: a 3D match with short lines in your chosen style. Broadcast is the producer's desk: calls, odds with reasons, and what to say next. Analyst is the workspace for numbers: season library, players, reports and the audit trail.

## Who it is for

A casual fan gets short lines with at most one number. An analyst gets the full numbers and the evidence behind each claim. A broadcaster gets lines they can read aloud in about a minute.
