title: Fouls and misconduct
---

## What counts as a foul

A foul is committed when a player challenges an opponent in a careless, reckless or excessive way, or handles the ball deliberately, while the ball is in play. Fouls inside the penalty area by the defending team are punished with a penalty kick.

## Cautions and sending off

A yellow card is a caution, for example for reckless challenges, time wasting or dissent. A red card sends the player off, for example for serious foul play, violent conduct, or denying an obvious goal-scoring chance by a foul. A second yellow card in the same match ends in a red.

## Advantage

The referee may let play continue if the team that was fouled benefits. If the benefit does not come, the referee can return to the foul. This is our own summary; the official text is in the IFAB Laws of the Game.
