---
title: Tackles won
---

## Definition

A tackle is an attempt to take the ball from an opponent who has it. A tackle is won when the tackler's team ends up in possession or the opponent loses control of the ball.

## How MatchCast measures it

`tackles_won:<team_id>` counts tackle events whose outcome is `won`. A lost or neutral tackle does not add to the count. The metric says nothing about how often a team tackled in total.

## Reading it

A high count can mean a team defends well or simply that it spends a long time without the ball. Compare it with possession and fouls: many tackles with many fouls suggests a rougher style, many tackles with few fouls suggests clean timing.
