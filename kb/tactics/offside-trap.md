---
title: The offside trap
---

## What it is

The offside trap is a defensive tactic where the back line steps forward together at the moment a pass is played, leaving an attacker ahead of the last defender and therefore offside.

## What it needs

Timing and communication. Every defender must move on the same cue, usually a call from the goalkeeper or a centre-back. If one defender stays back, the attacker is played onside and runs through on goal.

## Risks and rewards

A successful trap wins a free kick high up the pitch and wastes an attack. A failed trap can leave a forward alone with the goalkeeper. Teams that defend high and press hard use it most, because their back line is already close to the halfway line.
