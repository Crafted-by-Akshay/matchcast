title: Pressing
---

## What it is

Pressing means closing down the opponent when they have the ball so they cannot play comfortably. The aim is to win the ball back quickly, ideally close to the opponent's goal.

## High and low blocks

A high press starts in the opponent's half and demands a compact team that moves together. A low block waits near the defending team's own penalty area and gives up the ball in exchange for less space behind the defence.

## Pressure in MatchCast

The pressure index counts how many opponents are close to the player on the ball and how near they are. A high value means the player has little time and space to choose a pass.
